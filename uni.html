A few quick answers

Everything you need before you scan and go.

Who can join the mission?

Anyone. Download UltraStream and start completing missions, no matter who you are or where you're studying.

What's Stream Squad?

Stream Squad is UltraStream's campus ambassador programme. Members take the mission further on their own campus in exchange for bigger rewards and real CV experience.

Do I need to be a Stream Squad member to take part?

No. Every download and completed mission counts toward the Mission to a Million and the $1M AUD giveback pool. Stream Squad is an optional next step for students who want to go further.

Is Stream Squad only for university students?

Yes. You need to be 18 or over and currently enrolled at university to apply.
